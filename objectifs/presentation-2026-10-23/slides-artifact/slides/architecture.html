Architecture: deploy, test, reset

Deploy, test, reset. A scenario describes its devices, its services and weaknesses, and the allowed connections. It is deployed on Proxmox as virtual devices, running as LXC containers: web service, IoT service, other services, and a gateway with filtering. Ansible configures the services and resets them to a clean state. The audit agent plans and runs the tests against the virtual devices and exchanges instructions and observations with a local LLM server. The diagram is the one from the thesis committee presentation, with its labels translated into English.
