The sandbox launches a model or a script on a scenario. The model is local, on our own servers: Qwen3.8 27B, with a context of about 50,000 tokens, where hosted models offered 256,000 to one million. Because of this budget, the pipeline works per device, runs one test per weakness, keeps a compact context, and stores full traces outside it. The four modes run on the same scenario so that their results can be compared.
