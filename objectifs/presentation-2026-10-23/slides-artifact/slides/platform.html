The platform conducts the audit. Four execution modes: the LANCE pipeline (six phases, from graph analysis to report) and the rule-based audit produce a benchmark score; the simple agent and the script are the sandbox for a model or a script, with no deployment and no score. Scenarios come from a catalogue of 29 public scenarios with 21 vulnerability packs; the Scenario Lab generates variants. Models run locally; the cloud providers were removed from the code. The platform can prepare a rules-versus-LLM pair on one scenario; no paired result is shown here. Captures of the interface were taken on 22 October 2026.
