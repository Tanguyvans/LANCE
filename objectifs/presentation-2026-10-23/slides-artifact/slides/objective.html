Objectives

1 · Make the benchmark concrete

Compare LLM agents with declared scripts, at equal information, tools and budget, through six hypotheses.

2 · A sandbox for models and scripts

Run a local model or a script on the same scenarios and keep the evidence.

3 · Publish the results

A paper at ACM AsiaCCS 2027: under which conditions does an LLM agent add value over a script?

Three objectives. The benchmark comes first and the platform second; the paper reports what the benchmark measures and does not set the order of the work. The question is about conditions, not about which model wins. Model training is not a current priority.
