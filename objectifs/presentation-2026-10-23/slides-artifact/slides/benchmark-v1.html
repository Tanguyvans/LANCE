The catalogue has since grown to 29 public scenarios

Version 1 of the benchmark started with 12 scenarios, organised in six families according to the kind of access the audit has to examine. Counts are cumulative per family: 116 devices and 209 weaknesses in total. The scenarios are built from IoT topologies, configuration weaknesses and attack techniques catalogued in MITRE ATT&CK. The table is taken from the thesis committee presentation. The catalogue now holds 29 public scenarios, S1 to S29.
