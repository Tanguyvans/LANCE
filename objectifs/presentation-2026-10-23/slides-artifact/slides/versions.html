V1 is the reference state of the platform and its evaluation, dated 15 September 2026: 29 public scenarios, S1 to S19 for development and S20 to S29 for test. The test set is provisional: its independence from development was not verified, and the data are public. The score is the audit F1, and detection, proof, intrusion and consumption are never mixed into one global score. V2 was opened on 12 October 2026. Its question: in which situations does an LLM bring a useful gain over a competent conditional automation, at equal information, tools and budget? The result can be favourable, neutral or unfavourable to the LLM. It is organised as a register of hypotheses, H1 to H6, not a list of scenarios.
