V2: families vary by device, architecture and weakness

CategoryWhat variesExample variants
Devicesthe device under testMQTT formats, gateway, camera, sensor
Architecturehow the devices are connectedflat, segmented zones, gateway, star
Weaknessesthe type of flaw to findcredentials, data exposure, injection, crypto
V2 asks in which situations an LLM brings a useful gain over a competent script, at equal information, tools and budget. The benchmark is organised in three categories: devices, architecture and weaknesses. A scenario family varies one dimension inside a category; variants change one parameter and do not count as independent families. The hypotheses H1 to H6 stay in the register and each one is tested by a family. The first run is on MQTT formats, under devices. The V2 work started on 12 October 2026.
