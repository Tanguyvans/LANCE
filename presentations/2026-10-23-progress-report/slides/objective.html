Goal: a paper at ACM AsiaCCS 2027, submitted in 2026

The platform has two major parts. The benchmark and evaluation part holds the scenarios, each with known weaknesses, and scores every finding against them. The sandbox part is where a local model, a harness or a script is launched on the same scenarios, with the evidence kept. The overall goal is a paper at ACM AsiaCCS 2027: the question is under which conditions an LLM agent adds value over a script. Model training is not a current priority.
